# Catalog sources

`src/catalog/seed.json` is generated by `npm run build:seed` from the rows in `scripts/build-seed.mjs`. Edit the rows there, regenerate, and run `npm test` (the seed must pass catalog validation).

## Scope (catalog version 1)

Items unlocked at levels 1–30: all field crops, trees and bushes, chicken/cow/pig/sheep products and feeds, and the goods of the Feed Mill, Bakery, Dairy, Sugar Mill, Popcorn Pot, BBQ Grill, Pie Oven, Loom, Sewing Machine, Cake Oven, Smelter, Juice Press and Ice Cream Maker, plus expansion and storage materials, ores and fish fillet (listed as not produced). Goods above level 30 are not included yet; add them in the app's Catalog screen.

## How reliable it is

- **Names and unlock levels:** taken from the sources below; good confidence.
- **Times:** from the sources where they were listed (Bakery, Feed Mill, crops); otherwise best known values.
- **Recipe quantities:** public sources rarely list them in text. Bread, Corn Bread, Chicken Feed, Cookie, Raspberry Muffin, Blackberry Muffin and Sheep Feed were checked against at least one source; most others are best known values and **may be wrong**. Check the recipes you rely on and fix them in the app.
- Tree and bush harvests are set to 2 per harvest.
